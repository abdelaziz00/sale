How the data and forecasts work — read before trading

Market prices come only from the sources shown above. FUT.GG and FUTBIN do not publish public APIs: FUT.GG's robots.txt disallows /api/* and its Terms forbid automated access unless expressly permitted, and FUTBIN refuses automated clients. This dashboard therefore does not scrape them. You can connect a feed you are licensed to use (see config.json and the README), or log the prices you see on those sites with Log a price.

Forecasts are statistical estimates from each player's recorded history: a recency-weighted trend, momentum, distance from the 7-day median, listing supply (when available) and any promotions you add to data/events.json. They are not guarantees and are not financial advice. Confidence reflects fit quality, sample size, volatility and data age. Profit figures include the 5% EA tax.

Nothing on this page is invented: if a source fails, its status turns red, and any players it supplied show their last known timestamp.

Not affiliated with EA SPORTS, FUT.GG or FUTBIN. Forecasts are estimates, not advice.
